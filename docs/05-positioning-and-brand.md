# 05 · Positioning & Brand

Where the brand is today vs. the target for this rebuild, plus the proof points
available to support it.

## Current positioning (as-shipped)

- **Site title:** "AI Automation Consultant and Bionics Engineer."
- **Hero kicker:** "AI automation consultant · Bionics engineer."
- **Lede:** RAG systems, voice AI agents, n8n workflows, neurotechnology
  software → "reliable, measurable automation."
- **Proof grid:** 50K+ voice calls · 10+ engineers led · 3 products · 70% grant admin cut.
- **Credibility badges:** Upwork Top Rated Plus; certifications hidden.

The current site reads primarily as an **applied-AI / automation consultant**
with a strong **bionics/neurotech** research thread. The CTO role exists in the
experience data but is **not** the headline.

## Target positioning (this initiative)

Reframe the headline identity to **AI Engineer + Fractional CTO**, with the
existing automation-consulting and neurotech work as *supporting proof of depth*
rather than the lead message.

Suggested messaging spine (to validate with Mohamed):

- **Primary identity:** "AI Engineer & Fractional CTO" — I design, build, and
  lead the delivery of production AI systems, and I run the engineering function
  for founders who need a technical leader without a full-time hire.
- **What that means concretely (services):**
  - *Fractional CTO / technical leadership* — team leadership, architecture,
    delivery for AI-first products (backed by EXODIA CTO role + 10-engineer team).
  - *Applied AI engineering* — RAG platforms, LLM agents, voice AI, workflow
    automation (backed by shipped systems and measurable outcomes).
  - *AI advisory / consulting* — n8n, agents, integrations (backed by the
    consulting roster).
- **Supporting proof (depth & rigor):** neurotech/BCI research, GSoC, published
  paper, Cairo University + Sant'Anna pedigree.
- **Upwork:** stays a supporting credibility badge only, never the headline
  (consistent with CLAUDE.md).

## Gaps to close for the target positioning

- **No explicit "Fractional CTO" framing anywhere** in copy — the CTO role is
  buried in the experience list. Needs a dedicated hero line + services section.
- **No services / "work with me" section** — the site jumps from hero to
  project/experience lists. A fractional-CTO offer needs a clear
  engagement/services block and a stronger conversion path (call booking, not
  just mailto).
- **Proof metrics are hard-coded** and slightly inconsistent with CLAUDE.md and
  the experience data (e.g. "3 products shipped" isn't sourced). Make them
  data-driven and reconciled.
- **No case studies** — outcomes live as one-line summaries. Fractional-CTO
  buyers want 2–3 deeper case studies (problem → what I led → measurable result).
- **No testimonials / social proof** beyond the Upwork badge.
- **Academic thread is prominent** (publications/education high in the scroll);
  for the new positioning it should support, not lead.
- **No clear "who I help / when to hire a fractional CTO"** narrative for the ICP
  (early-stage AI founders, SMBs adding AI).

## Brand guardrails (keep)

- Don't make the homepage feel like an Upwork profile.
- Keep it business-focused, not academic-only.
- Keep proof metrics in the hero.
- Don't regress to the old classic-resume look.
- Preserve the neurotech/BCI credibility — it's a genuine differentiator among
  AI engineers.
